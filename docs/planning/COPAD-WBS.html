Company Overview
Who COPAD Is
1. Company Overview

COPAD Pharma Egypt at a glance.

Background

  • Founded in 1989, manufacturing and trading medicinal and veterinary products
  • Three divisions: Pharmaceutical, Wellness, Veterinary
  • GMP-aligned manufacturing facilities in El Obour City, including a dedicated pharmaceutical plant and a separate supplements/nutraceutical facility
  • Therapy areas: CNS, Ortho, Rheuma, CMD, Gyna/Andro, GIT, ENT, Pulmonology, Immunology
  • Registered and monitored under the Egyptian Drug Authority, NFSA, and MOH

Market Position & Direction

  • Currently perceived as No. 1 in CNS and Osteoarthritis/Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Ambition to move from a top-40 to a top-20 market position over 3–5 years
  • Consumer healthcare is the highest growth priority, followed by contract manufacturing, cosmeceuticals, and new therapy areas
  • Preparing for a secondary listing on the Egyptian Exchange, positioning investor confidence as a key communication priority
  • Main competitors: EVA, Lundbeck, Zeta

Brand Direction
Personality & Tone
2. Brand Personality, Tone & Audience

Direction established from the completed branding questionnaire.

Positioning

  • Brand words: Ethical, Expanding, Engaging
  • Traits: Trustworthy, Scientific, Innovative, Caring, Premium
  • Balanced midpoint: classic/modern, corporate/friendly, scientific/emotional, local/global
  • Must not feel like a classical, generalist pharma company

Tone of Voice

  • Clinical and precise — doctors, pharmacists, regulators
  • Warm and accessible — consumers and patients
  • Corporate-confident — investors and business partners
  • Always trustworthy, scientific, caring, premium underneath

Priority Audiences

  • 1. Consumers / patients
  • 2. Investors
  • 3. Doctors / distributors
  • 4. Regulators
  • 5. Future employees
  • 6. Export / business partners

Visual Direction

Evolution of the current logo rather than a completely new mark, preserving historical equity. Color palette open to creative recommendation. Reference points: Roche for pharma credibility, Haleon for consumer accessibility — the visual system flexes between clinical and consumer registers without splitting into two separate brands. All decisions respect EDA, NFSA, and MOH regulatory boundaries.

Native Website Development
9. Native Website — Scope of Work

A custom-built digital platform for COPAD.

The website will be developed as a professional corporate platform using PHP/Laravel or an equivalent custom framework, with a dedicated custom admin portal, built for stronger scalability, control, and long-term technical flexibility. It will support COPAD's corporate image, product portfolio, manufacturing credibility, business inquiries, and future commercial expansion.

Best Fit

  • When COPAD wants a custom admin experience tailored to internal workflows
  • When long-term scalability and security control are priorities
  • When the platform may later include advanced modules, integrations, dashboards, or custom business logic
  • When COPAD wants less dependency on third-party CMS/plugin limitations

9.1 Website Development Process

Phase 1: Website Objective Definition

Before design or development, the main objective of the website is defined — this is critical because the website should not only look modern.

The website should answer

  • What do we want visitors to see?
  • What do we want visitors to understand?
  • What do we want visitors to do?
  • Who are the main website audiences?
  • Is the website mainly for corporate credibility, product discovery, commercial inquiries, recruitment, or all combined?
  • How should COPAD be perceived after visiting the website?

Output

  • Website objective definition
  • Audience journey understanding
  • Key website communication priorities
